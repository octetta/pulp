# Future PolyBLEP Roadmap for Pulp & k/synth

This document captures the evaluation and future design considerations for extending PolyBLEP (and PolyBLAMP) across `pulp` and `ksynth`.

---

## 1. Context & Motivation

PolyBLEP (Polynomial Band-Limited Step) was initially introduced to `pulp` in `v0.81.0` to resolve hard-sync phase-reset aliasing (`HS`). Because PolyBLEP operates in the time domain at single sample rates without oversampling filters or multi-table mipmaps, it provides high-quality anti-aliasing with negligible CPU overhead (less than 0.05 operations per sample amortized).

Many waveforms in `pulp` originate in `ksynth` or are loaded as static wavetables. Expanding anti-aliasing cleanly requires harmonizing the offline vector generation domain (`ksynth`) and the real-time voice streaming domain (`pulp`).

## 2. Pulp Real-Time Audio Engine Extensions

### A. Wavetable & Loop Wrap Anti-Aliasing
* **Location**: [`parts/synth-osc.c`](file:///home/stewartj/book/pulp/parts/synth-osc.c) inside `osc_cycle_phase_next` and `osc_next_at`.
* **Mechanism**:
  - The oscillator wrap detection already computes sub-sample wrap fraction $d \in [0, 1)$:
    $$d = \frac{\phi - \phi_{\text{loop\_end}}}{\Delta\phi}$$
  - For any table or looped sample, measure the boundary step discontinuity:
    $$\Delta = \text{table}[\text{start}] - \text{table}[\text{end}]$$
  - If $|\Delta| > \epsilon$, add the 2-point PolyBLEP residual directly to `sv.sync_blep[voice]`:
    $$\text{residual} = \Delta \cdot \left(d - \frac{d^2}{2} - \frac{1}{2}\right)$$
* **Impact**: Immediately anti-aliases any single-cycle wavetable or unaligned sample loop generated by `ksynth` or loaded from disk, without modifying individual wavetable files or synthesis scripts. Smooth waveforms ($\Delta \approx 0$) bypass the calculation via a single float threshold check.

### B. Pure Analytic Virtual Analog (Saw, Square, PWM)
* **Location**: Dedicated analytic branch in `parts/synth-osc.c`.
* **Mechanism**:
  - Instead of looking up `WAVE_TABLE_SAW_UP` or `WAVE_TABLE_SQR` in 4096-sample unbandlimited memory tables, calculate:
    - Saw: $y(t) = 2\phi - 1 + \text{blep}(d)$ at cycle wrap.
    - Pulse/PWM: $y(t) = \text{sgn}(\phi - \text{duty}) + \text{blep}_0 - \text{blep}_{\text{duty}}$.
* **Impact**: Eliminates L1 cache misses and memory dereferences for standard synth waveforms, running entirely inside CPU registers while providing alias-free leads and continuous PWM.

## 3. k/synth Language Extensions

### A. PolyBLEP Verb / Alias (`bp` / `saw`)
* **Location**: [`parts/vendor/ksynth/ksynth.c`](file:///home/stewartj/book/pulp/parts/vendor/ksynth/ksynth.c).
* **Current State**: `saw` is aliased to `"o x"` (additive synthesis summing $N \times 32$ sines, requiring over $1.4\text{ million}$ operations per second of audio).
* **Proposal**:
  - Introduce a dyadic PolyBLEP primitive `P bp F` (phase vector `P` and phase increment `F`).
  - Update `saw` and `pulse` to evaluate naive modulo ramps followed by `bp`.
* **Impact**: Reduces k/synth generation time for saw/square audio buffers and drums by over 95%, while maintaining band-limited output.

### B. Internal Wavetable Player (`t`) Anti-Aliasing
* **Location**: Dyadic `t` verb handler in `ksynth.c:704`.
* **Proposal**: Track sub-sample wrap fraction during table traversal and apply PolyBLEP to table boundary discontinuities inside `t`.

## 4. Evaluation Summary

| Target | Discontinuity | Complexity | Priority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pulp Table/Loop Wrap PolyBLEP** | Step ($\Delta y$) | Low (~10 LOC) | **Immediate Next** (Harmonizes with all k/synth tables) |
| **Pulp Analytic Saw/Pulse/PWM** | Step ($\Delta y$) | Medium | High |
| **k/synth `bp` Verb / Fast `saw`** | Step ($\Delta y$) | Medium in `ksynth.c` | Medium (Accelerates k/synth buffer rendering) |
| **PolyBLAMP for CZ Phase Distortion**| Slope ($\Delta y'$) | Medium | Low (Casio grit is part of character) |
